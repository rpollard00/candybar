# Candybar

A one-player tabletop arcade cabinet, 3D printed, built around a 16-inch monitor, a BC-250 board and an ATX power supply. This glossary fixes the words for the cabinet and for the layout study that designs it.

## Language

### The cabinet

**Reference cabinet**:
The one-player New Astro City mini with an 8-inch screen, built by the maker known as MGS. It sets the look of this cabinet and nothing else.
_Avoid_: the Astro City (that is the full-size Sega cabinet), the MGS bartop (that is his MVS-U4 build)

**Shell**:
All the printed outer structure of the cabinet.
_Avoid_: case, enclosure, housing

**Massing**:
The shell as one undivided solid, used to settle the shape before the shell is divided into printable parts.
_Avoid_: mockup, envelope

**Deck**:
The low front part of the cabinet that the player's hands rest on. It is wider than the body and ends just behind the foot of the screen face.
_Avoid_: control box, base, tray

**Control panel**:
The removable plate that carries the lever and the buttons. It sits in an opening in the deck, with a border of deck around it.
_Avoid_: control plate, panel (alone), deck top

**Tile**:
One printed piece of a control panel. A panel is wider than the printer bed, so it is a lever tile and a button tile.

**Shelf**:
The flat strip of deck between the control panel and the foot of the screen face.
_Avoid_: shelf for the rear shelf (that is the underside of the head)

**Body**:
The part of the cabinet behind and above the deck, which holds the screen and the electronics.

**Screen face**:
The reclined front of the body, from the shelf up to the hood. It is the apron and, above it, the bezel plate.
_Avoid_: front face, bezel (for the whole face)

**Apron**:
The body-colored lower band of the screen face, between the shelf and the bezel plate. It lifts the screen.
_Avoid_: chin, riser

**Bezel plate**:
A removable black plate with one window, fitted in front of the monitor. There is one plate for each aspect ratio.
_Avoid_: bezel insert, mask, surround

**Window**:
The opening in a bezel plate through which the screen shows.

**Cheek**:
The part of each side wall that stands forward of the bezel plate, beside it.
_Avoid_: wing, side frame

**Sweep**:
The concave curve where the screen face meets the deck.

**Facet**:
A flat angled cut along an edge of the shell, in place of a sharp corner.
_Avoid_: chamfer, bevel

**Base facet**:
The facet of 45 degrees round the foot of the cabinet, where the shell meets the desk.

**Head**:
The part of the body above the rear shelf. It has the full width of the cabinet back to the rear, and the hood stands on it.
_Avoid_: upper rear, overhang

**Rear shelf**:
The underside of the head, where the head stands out from the narrower lower body: a flat facet of 45 degrees on each side and one on the rear.
_Avoid_: shelf (alone), roll, shoulder, rear slope

**Hip**:
The line where a side facet and the rear facet of the rear shelf meet, at each rear corner.

**Band**:
The facet of 45 degrees on the rear edge of each side of the cabinet, between the full width and the narrower lower body. The hood band is a different part.
_Avoid_: step

**Stroke**:
The outer edge of the band as the side view shows it: an upright stub at the desk, a kick, the lean, and a level run. It has straight lines only.

**Cut-in**:
One of the short level grooves that turn each lower rear corner. A cut-in is 1.5 mm deep and does not go through the wall.
_Avoid_: vent, slot (the slot is the opening of a cut-in in the wall)

**Hand-hold**:
One of the two recesses in the rear wall of the head. A person puts the fingers in them to lift the cabinet.
_Avoid_: grip, pocket

**Edge break**:
A round of about 1 mm on a sharp convex edge. It is the last modelling step, and it does not change the form. Paint holds on an edge that has it.
_Avoid_: fillet (for this round), chamfer

**Hood**:
The cap above the screen face.
_Avoid_: billboard, topper, marquee (for the whole cap)

**Hood band**:
The strip across the front of the hood that holds the two speaker grilles and the marquee.

**Marquee**:
The display in the middle of the hood band.

**Rear door**:
The removable panel in the rear wall. It carries the vents and nothing else. The compute module stands behind it on a fixed mount.

**Compute module**:
The BC-250 with its heatsink, fan and shroud, handled as one unit on a fixed mount behind the rear door.
_Avoid_: compute assembly, compute tray

**Recline**:
The angle of the screen face from vertical.
_Avoid_: tilt (that word is for the hood face)

**Sight line**:
The line from the player's eye to the center of the screen. The sight angle is how far this line points below horizontal.

### The layout study

**Hardware**:
A purchased part: the monitor, the BC-250, the power supply, the lever, a button.
_Avoid_: component

**Printable part**:
A part that is designed to be printed, with its real geometry.

**Envelope**:
A simplified solid that stands for a piece of hardware in the layout.
_Avoid_: placeholder, dummy, massing

**Keep-out zone**:
Space that must stay empty of other parts: for a plug, a cable bend, airflow, or a part that is not designed yet.
_Avoid_: clearance (that is a distance), reserve

**Trim**:
A visible plate set into the shell: a bezel plate, a speaker grille, the marquee window, the light strip.

**Measured**:
A dimension that was taken from the real part.

**Sourced**:
A dimension from a data sheet, a standard, or a community model, not yet confirmed on the real part.

**Provisional**:
A dimension that is a design choice or an estimate, which a test or a decision can change.

**Derived**:
A dimension that is calculated from other dimensions.
