# The reference cabinet sets the look only

The reference cabinet is built around an 8-inch 4:3 screen and small controls. This cabinet has a 16-inch 16:10 monitor, a full-size lever with eight buttons, a BC-250 that needs up to 235 W of cooling, and an ATX power supply. We take the look from the reference (hood, recessed bezel between cheeks, sweep, wide deck with facets) and decide the proportions and all practical details from this hardware. Do not scale the reference, and do not treat a difference in proportion as a defect.

## Consequences

The cabinet is wider and lower for its width than the reference, and its deck is deeper. Ventilation, the rear door and the inside are designed for this hardware, not copied.
