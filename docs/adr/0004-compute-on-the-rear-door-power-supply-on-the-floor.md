# Compute module on the rear door, power supply on the floor

Record 0005 replaces one part of this record: the compute module has a fixed mount behind the door and does not come out with it.

The BC-250 stands on its long edge against the rear door, with the heatsink toward the rear and the fan against a vent in the door. The power supply lies on the floor at the rear left, with its mains inlet through the rear wall. We chose this because the fan takes outside air directly, the heavy parts are low, and the compute module comes out with the door. A board that lies flat needs a deeper cabinet or loses the direct intake. A board that stands on its short edge is taller than the space behind a reclined screen allows.

## Consequences

The cabinet depth is set by the power supply, its plugs and the screen face, not by the board. The rear door must be longer than the 256 mm printer bed in one direction, so it is more than one printed part. Where the hot air leaves (through the door or through the left side wall) is not decided.
