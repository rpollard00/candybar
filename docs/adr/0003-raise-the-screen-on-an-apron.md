# Raise the screen on an apron under a centered bezel

The owner wants the image centered in the bezel and the sight line between 15 and 25 degrees below horizontal. A centered image at that height needs a screen face about 455 mm long. We keep the black bezel plate near-square, as on the reference, and put a body-colored apron about 100 mm long between the shelf and the bezel plate. The apron is not on the reference.

## Considered options

- One tall black bezel from the shelf to the hood. Rejected: about 180 mm of black above and below a 194 mm image.
- A low screen with the bezel at the shelf, as on the reference. Rejected: the sight line is 30 degrees or more.

## Consequences

The cabinet is about 624 mm tall. The apron can later carry the start, coin and menu buttons, which would let the control panel be less deep. The numbers depend on the player's eye position, which is an estimate until it is measured.
