# The compute module has a fixed mount, and the rear door is a cover

This record replaces one part of record 0004: the compute module does not come out with the rear door.

The BC-250 keeps its place and its direction. It stands on its long edge behind the rear door, with the heatsink toward the rear and the fan toward a vent in the door. It has a fixed mount inside the cabinet. The rear door carries the vents and nothing else. The shroud and the exhaust duct stay with the compute module and meet the door with a soft seal.

The owner decided this on 2026-11-11. A door with the board on it stays tied to the cabinet by the fan leads and the power cables, so it is hard to handle.

## Consequences

The fan still takes outside air directly. The door lifts off with no cable on it. The compute module needs a gap to the door for the seal, so its position in the fit study changes. A person reaches the board, the fan headers and the I/O end through the door opening.
